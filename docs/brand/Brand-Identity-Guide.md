## 1. Brand Essence

JERTINA is a premium, intelligent, and future-facing commerce technology brand built to serve real customers and real business operations.

### Brand Attributes
- Trustworthy
- Modern
- Precise
- Scalable
- Smart
- Human-centered
- Premium
- Efficient

---

## 2. Brand Positioning

JERTINA is positioned as a technology-led commerce platform that combines ecommerce, automation, and AI to improve the way customers discover, purchase, and manage products.

---

## 3. Logo System

### Primary Mark
A combination mark consisting of:
- a geometric symbol
- the JERTINA wordmark

### Approved Logo Usage
- Full-color horizontal lockup
- Full-color stacked lockup
- Symbol-only app icon
- Monochrome black version
- Monochrome white version

### Clear Space
Maintain a minimum clear space equal to the height of the symbol's inner stroke on all sides.

### Minimum Size
- Digital: 24 px width minimum for the full logo
- Print: 20 mm width minimum for the full logo
- Icon: 16 px minimum for the symbol

## 5. Typography

### Primary Typeface
Inter

### Fallback Stack
- system-ui
- sans-serif

### Usage
- Headings: Semi-bold to bold
- Body: Regular to medium
- UI labels: Medium

---

## 6. Icon Style

- Geometric
- Minimal
- Rounded where appropriate
- High contrast
- Scalable at small sizes

---

## 7. Brand Voice

- Clear
- Confident
- Helpful
- Direct
- Professional

Avoid:
- unnecessary jargon
- exaggerated claims
- playful tone in core product surfaces
