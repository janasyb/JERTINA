# Logo Specification

## JERTINA

**Version:** v0.1.0  
**Status:** Draft for Review  
**Milestone:** RF-005

---

## Logo Concept

A geometric symbol paired with a clean wordmark. The symbol is designed to remain legible at favicon scale and work independently of the wordmark.

---

## Approved Variants

1. Full-color horizontal logo
2. Full-color stacked logo
3. Symbol-only icon
4. Monochrome black
5. Monochrome white

---

## Usage Rules

- Never stretch the logo.
- Never alter proportions.
- Never recolor outside the approved palette.
- Never place on low-contrast backgrounds.
- Never add shadows or effects.
- Never crop the symbol or wordmark.

---

## Export Targets

- SVG
- PNG
- PDF
- Favicon
- App icon

---

## Safe Area

The safe area should remain clear of competing text, imagery, or UI components.

Recommended safe area:
- at least 1x symbol width on each side of the full lockup
