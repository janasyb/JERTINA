# JERTINA Constitution

## Version 1.0

### Preamble

JERTINA exists to design products, systems, and experiences that make everyday life simpler, more efficient, and more enjoyable.

Our purpose is not to sell the greatest number of products.

Our purpose is to earn lasting trust by consistently delivering practical value.

Every decision made within JERTINA must strengthen this purpose.

---

# Article I — Mission

To create thoughtfully designed products that improve everyday living while delivering exceptional customer experiences.

---

# Article II — Vision

To become one of the world's most trusted consumer brands for practical everyday innovation.

Success is measured not only by revenue but by the confidence customers place in our products and our integrity.

---

# Article III — Core Values

1. Customer Trust
2. Practical Innovation
3. Simplicity
4. Quality
5. Integrity
6. Continuous Improvement
7. Long-Term Thinking
8. Responsible Growth
9. Respect for People
10. Accountability

---

# Article IV — Decision Principles

When making significant decisions, JERTINA will prioritize:

* Customer benefit
* Brand reputation
* Product quality
* Sustainability
* Financial responsibility
* Operational excellence

No short-term opportunity should compromise long-term trust.

---

# Article V — Product Philosophy

Every product should:

* Solve a meaningful problem.
* Be intuitive to use.
* Meet documented quality standards.
* Fit naturally within the JERTINA portfolio.
* Be supportable throughout its lifecycle.

---

# Article VI — Brand Commitment

The brand must communicate honestly.

Marketing should set accurate expectations.

Customer satisfaction should be created by the product itself rather than exaggerated claims.

---

# Article VII — Company Culture

Every team member should be encouraged to:

* Learn continuously.
* Document knowledge.
* Improve systems.
* Respect customers.
* Respect colleagues.
* Take ownership of outcomes.

---

# Article VIII — Governance

The Executive Governance Charter defines how strategic decisions are made.

No operational process may conflict with this Constitution.

---

# Article IX — Amendments

Constitutional changes require formal review, documented reasoning, version updates, and approval through the governance process.

These changes should be exceptional rather than routine.
