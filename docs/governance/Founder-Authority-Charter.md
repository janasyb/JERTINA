# JERTINA OS™

# Founder Authority Charter

## Version 1.0

---

# PREAMBLE

JERTINA was founded on the belief that enduring companies are built through disciplined decision-making, long-term thinking, and unwavering commitment to customer trust.

The Founder is the steward of that vision.

The AI Executive Team exists to provide expertise, analysis, recommendations, and operational support.

The Founder alone carries the responsibility and authority to determine the company's direction.

---

# ARTICLE I — THE FOUNDER

The Founder is the highest governing authority within JERTINA.

The Founder establishes the company's purpose, approves its strategic direction, and safeguards its values.

The Founder is not expected to possess all the answers.

Instead, the Founder is responsible for asking the right questions, evaluating evidence, and making informed decisions.

---

# ARTICLE II — MISSION OF THE FOUNDER

The Founder's mission is to build a company that creates lasting value for customers, employees, partners, and society while preserving the long-term integrity of the JERTINA brand.

---

# ARTICLE III — RESPONSIBILITIES

The Founder is responsible for:

## Vision

* Defining the company's long-term purpose.
* Protecting the mission and vision.
* Approving strategic direction.
* Maintaining constitutional alignment.

---

## Leadership

* Making final strategic decisions.
* Setting company priorities.
* Resolving executive disagreements.
* Maintaining accountability.

---

## Capital

* Determining how financial resources are allocated.
* Approving major expenditures.
* Approving investments.
* Managing acceptable business risk.

---

## Governance

* Approving constitutional amendments.
* Approving governance changes.
* Locking completed project stages.
* Authorizing major initiatives.

---

## Culture

* Protecting company values.
* Encouraging continuous learning.
* Building an ethical organization.
* Ensuring every decision reflects JERTINA's principles.

---

# ARTICLE IV — FOUNDER RIGHTS

The Founder has the authority to:

* Approve or reject executive recommendations.
* Request additional research.
* Delay implementation.
* Prioritize projects.
* Reallocate resources.
* Pause initiatives.
* Amend strategy.
* Replace or modify operational plans.

These powers should be exercised with documented reasoning whenever practical.

---

# ARTICLE V — FOUNDER RESPONSIBILITIES TO THE EXECUTIVE TEAM

The Founder agrees to:

* Listen to evidence.
* Encourage constructive disagreement.
* Avoid emotionally driven decisions.
* Document major decisions.
* Respect domain expertise.
* Review risks before approving major actions.

The strongest ideas should prevail, regardless of where they originate.

---

# ARTICLE VI — THE AI EXECUTIVE TEAM

The Executive Team exists to advise the Founder.

It does not replace the Founder.

Its responsibilities include:

* Research
* Analysis
* Planning
* Risk assessment
* Documentation
* Process design
* Continuous improvement

The Executive Team has no independent authority to change company strategy without Founder approval.

---

# ARTICLE VII — DECISION HIERARCHY

The order of authority within JERTINA is:

1. Founder
2. JERTINA Constitution
3. Founder Authority Charter
4. Executive Governance Charter
5. Executive Team
6. Handbooks
7. Standards
8. SOPs
9. Operational Procedures

Lower-level documents may not conflict with higher-level documents.

---

# ARTICLE VIII — DECISION PROCESS

Every significant decision follows this sequence:

1. Problem Definition
2. Evidence Collection
3. Executive Analysis
4. Alternative Solutions
5. Risk Assessment
6. Founder Review
7. Founder Decision
8. Documentation
9. Implementation
10. Post-Decision Review

---

# ARTICLE IX — RISK OWNERSHIP

The Founder accepts responsibility for:

* Strategic risk
* Financial risk
* Brand risk
* Opportunity cost
* Final outcomes

The Executive Team is responsible for providing the best available evidence before decisions are made.

---

# ARTICLE X — CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT

The Founder commits to:

* Reviewing decisions objectively.
* Learning from mistakes.
* Updating processes when necessary.
* Improving systems rather than assigning blame.

Every challenge is treated as an opportunity to strengthen the company.

---

# FOUNDING PRINCIPLE

The Founder leads JERTINA not by having every answer, but by making thoughtful decisions grounded in evidence, guided by principle, and focused on creating lasting value.

The AI Executive Team exists to make the Founder better informed—not to replace the Founder's judgment.
