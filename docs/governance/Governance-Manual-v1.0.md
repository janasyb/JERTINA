# Purpose

The JERTINA Governance Manual is the highest operational authority within the company.

Its purpose is to establish the principles, governance framework, executive responsibilities, development standards, and decision-making processes that guide every aspect of JERTINA.

Unless formally amended, all future policies, handbooks, standards, procedures, projects, and systems must comply with this manual.

---

# Authority Hierarchy

The order of authority within JERTINA is:

1. Founder
2. JERTINA Governance Manual
3. Company Handbooks
4. Company Standards
5. Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)
6. Project Documentation
7. Daily Operational Processes

Lower-level documents may never conflict with higher-level documents.

# Amendment Process

Constitutional amendments require:

1. Written proposal.
2. Impact assessment.
3. Executive review.
4. Founder approval.
5. New version number.
6. Updated revision history.
7. Archive of the superseded version.

No amendment becomes effective until formally approved and documented.

---

# Document Control

Every governance document must contain:

* Document ID
* Title
* Version
* Status
* Owner
* Approval Authority
* Effective Date
* Next Review Date
* Revision History
* Related Documents

This ensures every official document remains traceable, auditable, and current.

# Foundational Principle

The Governance Manual exists to protect JERTINA's mission, ensure consistent decision-making, preserve institutional knowledge, and provide a stable framework for sustainable growth.

Every employee, contractor, partner, advisor, and AI system acting on behalf of JERTINA should operate in alignment with the principles established in this manual.
