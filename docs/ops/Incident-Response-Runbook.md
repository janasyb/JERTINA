# Incident Response Runbook

## JERTINA

**Version:** v0.1.0  
**Status:** Draft for Review  
**Milestone:** RF-020

---

## Severity Levels

### Sev 1
Complete outage or active data loss.

### Sev 2
Major degradation with partial service impact.

### Sev 3
Limited feature impact or non-critical failure.

### Sev 4
Minor issue or follow-up task.

---

## Response Steps

1. Confirm the incident.
2. Identify affected systems.
3. Contain the issue.
4. Restore service or data.
5. Communicate status.
6. Document the resolution.
7. Review and improve prevention controls.

---

## Escalation
- Technical escalation goes to the engineering team.
- Business-critical issues escalate to the founder.
- Security incidents are handled with least disclosure and immediate containment.

---

## Post-Incident Review
Every Sev 1 and Sev 2 incident requires a post-incident review with:
- timeline
- root cause
- corrective action
- preventive action
